              Your bat

              How to win

              Every bat brings three moves. In each round, two bats face each other and play their moves one after the other. Each move beats one and loses to one:

              • Swoop beats Screech: it dives before the screech lands.
              • Screech beats Dodge: you can't dodge sound.
              • Dodge beats Swoop: the diver hits only air.

              The bat that wins more of the three moves takes the round; a tied round goes to whichever bat the wind favours. Five rounds, and the group with more rounds wins. A team flies at most 5 bats, picked fresh each week. In a small team the same bat flies several rounds, starting its moves one step later each time, so a big group has no advantage, and nobody's cave or calcite plays any part. Nobody sees anyone's moves before the fight, not even their own teammates'.
